Ten stages, one road trip

One continuous drive, from dawn on a seaside boulevard to sunset on a coastal island two days later. Beat the clock to each checkpoint, and every second you save rides on to the next stage.

  1. Stage 1

    Ocean Boulevard

    Dawn on the seafront. Palms, towers and the first traffic of the day.

  2. Stage 2

    The Causeway

    A road across open water: a town on the sea, a long bridge and a glass tunnel under the waves.

  3. Stage 3

    Golden Fields

    Rolling hills, sunflowers, and an eight-lane road that drops straight into the valley.

  4. Stage 4

    Vineyard Hills

    Narrow roads through the vines at dusk, a wine village and a chateau on the hill.

  5. Stage 5

    Dark Forest

    The one night of the trip. Headlights, a hidden lake, and a long road into the moon.

  6. Stage 6

    Old Town

    First light on the cobblestones. Half-timbered houses, a cathedral and the old stone bridge.

  7. Stage 7

    High Pass

    Two lanes, falling snow and a long drop at the edge. The climb to the top of the trip.

  8. Stage 8

    Red Canyon

    Red walls, a green river below, and then the canyon opens onto a vast valley.

  9. Stage 9

    Sand Sea

    Dunes to the horizon and a sandstorm on the last climb. Then, over the top, the sea.

  10. Stage 10

    Sunset Bay

    The widest road of all, a great bridge over the bay, and a victory lap into the sunset.
